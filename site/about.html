About

One free appfor your audiobook.

Import the book. Add the recording. Kosmos proofreads the words and exports files Audible will take. It runs on this computer. No account. No monthly fee.

What you use it for

Most people buy this in pieces: a paid teleprompter, a paid proofreading upload, then another tool to hit Audible’s numbers. Kosmos is all of that in one download.

You get a page that follows as you read, a list of missed words you can play back, and a finished folder of named files ready to upload. Authors and narrators can work the same book together — including on hotel Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot.

What Kosmos is not

  • 01
    Not a replacement for Reaper Record and edit in the software you already use. Kosmos sits next to it.
  • 02
    Not a performance coach It catches words that do not match the book. You still listen for acting, clicks, and room noise.
  • 03
    Not an AI narrator It will not read the book or clone a voice. This is for a human recording.

$0open source

Mac and Windows. Source on GitHub. The unpublished book stays on this computer.
